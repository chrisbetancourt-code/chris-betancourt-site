About

One calling, four ways to serve.

I'm Chris: a youth pastor, worship leader, event director and musician, based in Orlando and glad to travel. I treat all four as one calling, helping people meet Jesus and find a place to belong in a church family.

Whether I'm teaching, leading a song, building a schedule or playing a part on guitar, I come prepared, work well with your team, and care about the people in the room more than the performance.
